
# Intertidal Design Doc

Speculative design doc for a visual novel game that might be pitched to Games for Change -- Olive R.F. September-October 2026

Its been broken down into a bunch of docs in this folder.

## Themes:

How do we cope? Through incremental change, getting in touch with natural cycles.
- Agency changing with the Tides could be read as a metaphor for fluctuating capacity from living with health problems: trauma; disabilities; chronical illness.
- What kinds of connection?
- Subjectivity of time
- Feeling caught (even preyed upon) by flowing attention-hooked states
- Interested in habits (ritual and smart phone usage) and how micro-choices can add up to change your life.

### Big feelings! 
This game deals with tough subject matter based on real lived experience of growing up through the dusk of the Anthropocene, and the drawn-out death of the Internet.
The average experience of living through PermaCrisis for our generation seems to be treading water in interconnected tragedies, to feel incredibly inundated with information and starved for quality connection.

Some negative emotions we're drawing on (might be worth putting a content warning) include:
- **Always being on the backfoot** there's never enough time for everything you want to do
- ghosting and social overwhelm
- alienation
- self-reliance
- lack of agency
- fatigue, apathy, anxiety from constant bad news
- addiction to flowing experiences like Feeds.

But moving towards:
- connection
- subtle time

## Gameplay

Complementing a narrative structure about repetition, routine, making you feel small at first but then finding iterative change in little choices you can make daily.

**Considering how you exercise your agency in small ways by how use your spare time and attention, and through intentional use of technology.**

Mechanics:
- an everyday choice between action and inaction
- moving between scenes
	- **not always by choice**	
- Time as a limited resource
	- A visual indicator of how much time you have left
	- occasional little “glimmers” of novelty if you do decide to start to commit to a routine.
	Interesting dialogue system where you select from “shorthand” options and then fake-type them your keyboard (very juicy)
	There are sometimes "fakeout" dialogue options that aren't consistent with what you intended to type and reveal your anxieties or your masking: Emulates the room for rumination that is inherent online socialisation.

- Based on real life moon cycles.
	- What happens if you cheat the lunar cycle? Burnout?

### Attention Hooks
- different visuals/UI with varying levels of **juice**
- creation of a flow state when you're in a spiral

### Core Experience

We play this game in the protagonist's downtime, and in our own downtime. It asks us to question each day: **what do you do with the feeling that your time is finite, and that you're always on the back-foot?**

**Changes to routine are small and incremental:** you can gradually shape your habits and find that time is more subtle when you're out exploring the world and connecting with people.

Each day you are presented with choices and a lot of them are dead-ends -- you are finding the signal in the noise that is the daily inundation of information.

The story takes place over a lunar cycle - whether that is a full or a half-cycle is uncertain at this point. **It starts at Last-quarter, coming up on Dark-Moon.

We are considering that the story takes place in real-time as well, only allowing a short play-time each day, and linked to the real Lunar cycles.

## Loop?

- With your eyes closed, check in with yourself for the day.
	- Maybe set an intention?
- Follow "signals" dialogue branches to find Events.
- Follow "noise" dialogue branches to lose Time
- Move between scenes if the tides are strong enough


### Time and Tides

- Your spare time in **each day is a finite resource**
- Time represented as an integer (e.g. 30), counting down to zero with each dialogue option.
- It is your **habitual routine to use this time up** by getting caught in Siphon-spirals. 
- When you've dipped below 0, you reach the end of the current dialogue branch and that's your spare time gone for the day.
- The strength of the tides changes with the moon phases, reaching their **highest at Bright-Moon / Dark-Moon** (Spring Tides), and their **lowest at Quarter-Moons** (Neap Tides).
- Spring Tides = longer, more significant days, 
	- You have these days off work.
	- **Signals reach you from farther away**:
		- Get in contact with distant friends like the Solarpunk.
	- You can **travel farther into deeper/shallower** scenes
		- Dive down and meet folks from the Fringes, or catch a stream up to the Shallows.

Thematically, this contributes to feelings of overwhelm and lack of agency -- **there are forces at play greater than you.**

### Signal/Noise

**Signal**: dialogue options which persist.
**Noise**: dialogue options which shuffle, disappear after you select them, and are inconsequential attention-grabs.

**Some Signals are pervasive Attention Hooks**, a Siphon baiting you into a spiral. Examples might be advertising and outrageous headlines. Once you're two steps deep into a dialogue tree from an Attention Hook, you can't escape it until it's sapped a significant amount of time from you for the day (equivalent to a long and draining social media scroll or internet rabbit-hole). **Attention Hooks are common in the Depths, where you start off most days.**

Inversely, connections with real people (that you might find in the Middle-depths, or Fringes) might catch your attention but not sap your energy from you in your curiosity.

Time is more subtle and after an interesting engagement with someone, you'll still have time in the day left, to go look at the sky.

### Events 

- Each event has a **duration based on lunar phases**. 
	- Some are specific: e.g. "I'll be there on First Quarter" gives you only one day.
	- Some are general: e.g. "you can catch me while the moon's waxing" gives you 14 days from Dark-Moon to Bright-Moon.
- There's **no UI to assist you in remembering events** -- 
	- "Bad" design on purpose -- this might be contentious.
	- like old text-game tropes of having to juggle lots of things in your mind.
	- this is analogous to the struggles of scheduling when you live with disabilities.
- Characters might ask you last-minute to **reschedule, creating clashes** with other things you had planned.


### Fluctuating tides

- Some days (particularly low) are write-offs regardless of your intention for the day. Because of this, **you inevitably miss things** you might have had planned. 
	- This is analogous to the reality of living with chronic illness/disability.
- If you've been doing a lot, you might have a zero-energy day coming up, even if you had things planned. On a day like this you can just take a walk, but don't have capacity for anything new or social.
- It's inevitable that you get caught by **Attention Hooks** on days like this, regardless of if you were meaning to or not.
	- Or perhaps there's a lot less options presented to you than the "easy" attention-catching ones.



### Mechanics that will probably be scrapped...

- Finite playtime each day
	- Kinda creates FOMO because you can waste it.
- There's a dice roll for how much energy you have on a given day
	- It might be based on how energy-expending your interactions of the previous day
