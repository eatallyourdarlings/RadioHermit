# Script 

if there's an inner-monologue that communicates a feeling of smallness then it could be in second-person, or it could be using "it/its" pronouns in third person to show how they narrativise their own  experiences of alienation. 

texting styles: all lowercase, using abbreviations like "ur" and "wtf"

Accepting Sola's invite:

*You follow a friendly current to the silty shallows, where you spot the sender picking over piles of discarded shells. Their shell is a lot smaller than you've ever seen, like they couldn't even fit inside of it. It has a mane of green chlorophyllic anemone that softly diffuse the light, and must have something to do with how they can walk about underwater. It looks like they're working on patching up a similar, small, shell with little spiral calcifications.* 

Stepping out of your shell into the Shallows:

*You almost forgot what it felt like to walk! Many folks here wear weathered old shells, having matured into security with one. Some even have the antennae broken off -- do they even still get caught in the radio-currents?*


Receiving an unexpected message from Old Friend:

*I appreciate you reaching out, and getting me out of The Depths for a while. The distance has been really hurting, though, it can't feel like coming up for air each time we see each other.*
