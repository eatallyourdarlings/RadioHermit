# Game Design Pillars

## Against the Flow
- The dialogue system is critical of predatory "flow-inducing experiences" like social media feeds: 
    - Attention-Hook dialogue branches are "juicy" and more fun, while dialogue with real people with real people is awkard and fumbly.
- Embrace a fear of missing out, with the narrative addressing days off.
- **Fluctuating agency with the Tides** means that you're not always able to do what you planned in a day.
    - Could be read as a metaphor for living with health problems: trauma; disability; chronical illness.

## Small Ripples Within Tidal Forces
- **Incrememental changes** through intentional use of technology, ritual and routine.
- **Finding subtle time** with very short daily play sessions, you might reconsider your power and relationship to time.
- **Little first steps into finding community** through art, activism, mutual aid.


## More-Than-Human Connection
- **Symbiopunk:**, a world built on interspecies reciprocity with no inorganic technology. Mirrors our power imbalances in our societies.
- **Reconsidering your relationship with technology** - how it augments your life. 
- **Ecology facilitates connection between humans**. Exploring how the Internet is an extension of our Ecology!
