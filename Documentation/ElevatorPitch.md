# Elevator Pitch

*InterTidal* is a durational narrative game targeting mobile phones that takes place in semi-real time over the course of a lunar cycle. You play in short daily sessions, determing what our protagonist Spira does with their finite downtime by controlling the smart-phone like interface to their radio-conch-shell. The game takes place in a **symbiopunk underwater city** where, to this version of humanity, their Internet is a mega-cities under the ocean, whose citizens mostly live in these shells which are analogous to our digital interfaces.

Spira is a lonely, queer and disabled young adult who is learning to **make small incremental changes in their life** to move out of alienation and try to thrive, not just survive, the overwhelm of the Polycrisis. Throughout the narrative cycle they rethink their relationship with the internet, seek community and move out of their comfort zone into gentler waters or deeper connections. 

In short: InterTidal is akin to a Twine or Visual Novel format RPG with a small scope of branching paths, that we'd describe as a "social-media alienation simulator that you play over a month".
