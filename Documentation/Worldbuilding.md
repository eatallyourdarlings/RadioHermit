# Worldbuilding


In InterTidal, humans inhabit an ocean planet living more symbiotically with no separation between culture and nature. With their own organic internet with similar power structures to ours, comes exploitative design and the relational problems inherent to that. To these folks, the Ocean is the Internet, and there's lots of different colloquial terms that they use to describe it, like *worrywaves*.

*What kind of fantasy?* It can’t be escapism; worldbuilding for the sake of worldbuilding; but a strong metaphor centered around connection.

- **Symbiopunk:** no inorganic technology. Beyond biopunk, a term coined by Audax 
- **Integration/cyborgism:** we see a spectrum of humans with different relationships with their shells, with meaningful  proportions between Human-Shell. 
- **Not mermaids:** these humans are still mammals best suited for land, and their symbiotic technology is overall a bit awkward and un-ergonomic.

## Themes

With less exposure to the attention-draining noise and more connection, you feel more **agency for change.**
Connection with local ecology facilitates connection between humans.

Through intentional use of the Internet, you can discover the varied and empowering community responses to local crises like pollution, Data Centres, surveillance and rise of policing. There is resistance if you look for it! By the end of the story, our protagonist is taking their first steps into Activism and Mutual Aid.

### interesting phenomena...

**carcinoecium** - a chitin on the outside of a shell that is produced by an attached sea anemone in a symbiotic relationship with a hermit crab.

[Kleptoplasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kleptoplasty) - sea-slugs are solar-powered through this symbiotic relationship where they eat but don't entirely digest photosynthetic algae, and it is used to **form complex patterns in their skin**.

**intertidal** - "between tides". **intertidal zone** is the middle depths of the ocean, between the high and lowest part of the shore: the beach exposed at maximum ebb spring tides. a.k.a. foreshore.

**Marine Noise Pollution** - Sound travels five times faster in water than in air. Mammals are particularly susceptible to and overwhelmed by this.

**stridulation** - the creation of acoustic waves through body parts adapted for rubbing together and creating a shrill sound: crickets, grasshoppers and strawberry hermit crabs all use this to communicate.

**sonification** - turning data into sound.

**tidal power** - potential energy of the movement of waves.

## RadioConch

Our generation's cyborgism swapped out for symbiopunk, living inside of shells that connect us through the ocean-internet. *We forget we aren’t pelagic organisms when we spend so long submersed.*

Hermit-shell metaphor for a constantly changing performance of digital identity (also tech redundancy).

The loneliness of that echo chamber... what if online was your only home? Your rock in the storm?

There's lots of different scales to the shell technology. Luddites you meet at the surface might only have the equivalent of a flip phone with an antenna that you pull out with your teeth.

*When you listen to a conch shell, you are listening to the blood flow inside of your own head. You might hear the ocean. Both of these things can be true. There is an ocean inside of us.*

Shell culture is communicated through silhouettes...
- Deeper = bigger and spikier shells! More maximalist, more antennae, customisedd to each personality.
- Medium = less variety in shells; uniform and "production-line". Human-shell proportions are closer to equal, but they still fit totally inside of them.
- Shallow = Smoother shells; less antennae. These folks are mostly offline, and much bigger than their shells if they have them. They're treated more like optional accessories: fannypacks; shoulder-bags. Nomadic, not tied to a particular shell.


## Protagonist

Our protagonist is of our generation; sensitising themself after a lifetime of exposure to the Depths.

They live with chronic illness/disabilities that mean that some days are write-offs.

They're queer as subtext but don't think there'll be romance in their life, perhaps just little flirting/crushes.

They work in the equivalent of a work-from-home tech job, their RadioConch always plugged in, not feeling like somewhere that they can fulfillingly work or rest.

## Locations

Different levels of depth in the ocean represent different kinds of online connection.

The Middle-depths and deeper are an incredibly dense underwater citiy of organic internet infrastructure.

7 stages of depth, you start at level 4: Siphons.

0. Underair (on land)
1. Surface - floating
2. Shores - just under surface
3. Midways
4. Siphons - where you start most days
5. Depths
6. Fringes

### Underair

Atolls of little squatter-communities trying to live apart from the hustle and bustle of the underwater metropoli. Dead Siphons wash up here and folks scavenge from their data-pollution.

You could wander these Atolls of you own volition if you take a big walk on the **first night of the Dark-Moon**.

You are invited up here by Activists on the **Bright-Moon Community Potluck**.


### Surface

Here you can float around just under the surface and look at the sky. A nice place to meet up with a friend, and maybe brave the open air together.
Folks here are in transition to slower ways of living and communicating... (Snail-email? snail-boats?)


### Shallow waters

Shallow Waters = Local-focused, low-tech communities. 
Discarded shells and quiet kelp gardens, where you **meet the Solarpunk.**


### Midways

The wide-open web and commute-network; turbulent waters of information overwhelm; noisy marine-snow.

Analogous to common internet pathways as well as to public transport networks in big cities. There are currents/desire paths in the Midways to get caught in and learn about things at a slower pace -- you might catch a signal that feels easier to hold your attention on to than if you were hooked by a Siphon.

You can drift through communities of shells tied together, but their shared tethers ultimately lead down into the Depths to a Siphon that they keep themselves mutually attached to (analogous to e.g. Fandom forums or Discord communities)



### Siphons

**This is where you start most days, coming back from work.**

Monolithic Siphonophores with floating attention-hooks. Looks like skyscrapers + electrical pylon megastructures + Colonial Organisms (Siphonophorae). 

- Equivalent to big-tech platforms, social media, 
- Also analogous city-centres of commerce, being on a train with everyone around you on their phones.
- Your work has you attached to these, and when 

Your time submerged in the other two middle-depths scenes always feels precarious for how there might be bioluminescent Attention Hooks that can drag you into the Depths.

The Siphons = Social Media Platforms (e.g. Instagram, Bluesky)

**Attention Hooks** floating around with millions of shells floating on tethers. This is infrastructure often mistaken for being Monolithic, is actually a symbiotic and dynamic system in itself, but more akin to parasitism than mutualism. Permanent storms form around them, marine snow always spiralling around them.

(also something something data colonialism)



### Depths

Often when you're in the Depths you forget that you took a dive in the first place seeking connection with other humans, with your attention so flooded by everything else.

Here you're exploring a **seemingly endless feed of branching paths** that feel labyrinthine and aren't the same each time you loop around. These are based on social media posts and headlines, and are broken-up and fragmented, simulating the experience of being in a hyper-fixated flow on something like Instagram. 

In the Depths, **signals are rarer**, but these opportunities for connection do stick out amidst the noise.

The Depths are analogous Deep Web/Algorithmic Rabbit Holes. Taking a Dive is going into an Internet Hole. Not necessarily bad diving into the Depths and can be done consensually (without being caught by an Attention Hook). But since the norm is to do so tethered to one of the Siphons, so allowing yourself to only go where you are guided by them, caught in deep-sea eddies (algorithm spirals). You see others around you doing the same and you feel a fleeting connection, but you’re trapped spiralling in parallel.


### Fringes

The Fringes are the undergrowth of the Depths: **shells and buoys tied together** to weather the Depths in online communities, forming **bridges between Siphons**. You can find **interesting fringe communities** (queer, underground artists and subversive characters). If our protagonist explores and makes connections here, then by the end of the story cycle they've started to find their own little niche where they fit in.

Folks at The Fringes live on the **edge of the Drop**: the deepest Depths / dark web / impassable edges of the known world.
