
## Primary mechanics ideas


Complementing a narrative structure about repetition, routine, making you feel small. iterative change in little choices you can make daily.

Foundational ideas:
- moving between scenes
	- **not always by choice**
- passing of time as a resource
	- by which I mean, every action takes x minutes
	- feeling of **always being on the backfoot** there's never enough time for everything you want to do
- different visuals/UI with varying levels of **juice**
- creation of a flow state when you're on your own, catching signals
- time feeling more subtle when you are connecting with someone.
- A visual indicator of how much time you have left.

More radical ideas...
- Based on real life moon cycles
- Can only play a bit once per day
- There's a dice roll for how much energy you have on a given day
	- It might be based on your interactions of the previous day
